STEAM DECK INSTALLATION
=======================

1. Double-click "Install Steam Deck File Transfer".
2. Choose "Execute" if SteamOS asks what to do.
3. After installation, open the new "Steam Deck File Transfer" shortcut on
   your Desktop and press "Start receiver".

No terminal, password, developer mode, or SteamOS system changes are needed.

Keep transfers on a trusted private home network. This alpha does not have
encrypted pairing yet.
